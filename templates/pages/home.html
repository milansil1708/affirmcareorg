Who We Are

Affirm Care is a mission-driven platform advancing LGBTQ+ health equity with focused support for transgender, gender-diverse, and intersex communities. We connect people to affirming providers, practical education, and community-centered resources so care is easier to access, safer to navigate, and grounded in dignity.

We envision a future where every LGBTQ+ person receives equitable, respectful, and affirming healthcare without discrimination. Through trusted provider access, practical tools, and ongoing learning, we aim to close care gaps and help build a health system that works for everyone.
Our mission is to improve health outcomes for LGBTQ+ communities by making affirming care more visible and easier to find. We support this through provider directories, educational content, community-informed insights, and programs that strengthen trust between patients and healthcare systems.

Our Objectives

  • Improve the visibility of LGBTQ+ affirming providers through a trusted directory experience.
  • Expand access to quality services by organizing provider and care-navigation information in one place.
  • Support healthier conversations around diversity, identity, and intersectionality in care settings.
  • Encourage collaboration between community members, advocates, and provider organizations.
  • Deliver inclusive LGBTQ+ training resources for clinicians, educators, and students.
  • Promote research that improves policy and practice at the intersection of identity and health equity.
  • Partner with institutions to integrate LGBTQ+ topics into healthcare curricula.
  • Create mentorship pathways that support growth, leadership, and culturally responsive care.
  • Strengthen provider communities through training, practical resources, and peer learning.
  • Share patient-centered tools that help teams deliver affirming, high-quality care.
  • Support professional development through events, networking, and best-practice exchange.
  • Offer consultation support to help providers improve clinical understanding and service quality.
